Welcome to the Loft

Where historic charm meets rustic industrial style: exposed brick, arched windows, and the original hardwood floors.

The Loft at 708 is a one-of-a-kind event space on the second floor of the Miller Building, in the heart of downtown Huntingdon. Dress it up for a wedding or keep it rustic and relaxed for a birthday, a shower, or a holiday party. Your event, your way.

Downtown Huntingdon

Right in the middle of everything

The Loft sits above a tavern and beside a mini golf and arcade, on a walkable block of downtown Huntingdon. They are their own businesses, but they are steps away, and that changes what an evening can be.

Rehearsal, then mini golf

Host the rehearsal dinner upstairs, then walk the wedding party next door for a round. Same building, nobody moves a car.

A room for the kids

Book a kids’ room with the arcade next door, and the parents can stay through the last dance.

A party that spills over

The graduation party is in the hall while the cousins drift into the arcade and back. Nobody is bored.

Lunch with something after

A corporate lunch with mini golf or the arcade next door afterward. One address, no caravan to a second venue.

Dinner is a block away

Restaurants within a block and a tavern one floor down. Guests walk to dinner and walk back.
