For your guests

Where to stay, eat, and park

Send this page to your guest list. Everything here is within a few blocks of the hall in downtown Huntingdon, with the walkable options marked and the addresses ready to copy into a phone.

Stay

A place to rest

One place you can walk to, two a short drive out. Book early for peak-season Saturdays.

Gage Mansion B&B Penn Street · about 4 blocks

A restored Victorian inn, and the closest bed to the hall. It has only a handful of rooms, so reserve it first for the wedding party.

Walkable
Comfort Inn S 4th Street · about 0.6 mi

Reliable rates and free parking, a five-minute drive or a fifteen-minute walk downhill into town.

Short drive
Fairfield by Marriott Route 22 · about 2.5 mi

The newest rooms and the largest room block, out on the highway. Best for guests driving in who want easy parking and a quick morning exit.

Drive
Eat

A table nearby

Breakfast, a late drink, or somewhere to send the out-of-towners the night before, all on foot.

Muddy Run Tavern Same building, ground floor

Downstairs from the hall. A separate business, and the most convenient last drink in town.

One floor down
Sazon Latina Washington Street · across the street

Latin plates directly across from the front door. Good for a relaxed rehearsal lunch or feeding early arrivals.

Walkable
Plus a block of more Within one block

Coffee, pizza, and more within a block on Washington and Penn. Ask your event attendant for a current short list.

Walkable
Getting here

The Miller Building, second floor

Getting up
The Loft is on the second floor of the Miller Building at 708 Washington Street. Look for the black door with our name in gold.
Parking
Metered street parking on Washington Street is free after 6pm and on weekends. A municipal lot sits behind the building, a half-block walk from the door.
Drop-off
Pull up right out front on Washington Street for anyone who would rather not walk from the lot. Rideshare works well in town and back to the hotels.
