Where to stay, eat, and park

Send this page to your list. Everything below is within a few blocks of the hall in downtown Huntingdon — the walkable options are marked, and the addresses are here to copy straight into a phone.

Stay

One place you can walk to, two a short drive out. Book early for peak-season Saturdays.

Gage Mansion B&B

A restored Victorian inn, the closest bed to the hall. Small — a handful of rooms — so reserve it first for the wedding party.

The address

708 Washington Street
Second floor, Huntingdon PA

Getting up
Elevator and stairs from the Washington Street entrance to the second floor. The elevator is ADA accessible — guests using it come up the same way as everyone else.
Parking
Metered street parking on Washington is free after 6pm and on weekends. A municipal lot sits behind the building, a half-block walk to the door.
Drop-off
Pull up right out front on Washington for anyone who’d rather not walk from the lot. Rideshare works well in town and back to the hotels.
