What moves it

Three things set your number.

Guest count decides how much room and how many linens. Bar service is the biggest swing: a hosted full bar for 200 can cost more than the room itself, while a cash bar costs you nothing. And the day of the week matters, because a Saturday in June is our busiest slot and a Tuesday in February our quietest. Tell us those three and we will give you a real number, not a range.

The terms
Reservation fee
A non-refundable fee holds your date. It is applied to your balance, not added on top, so it is money toward your event rather than a deposit you lose.
Event insurance
A day-of event policy is required and purchased by you. It runs about $100 to $200 through most providers, and we can point you to one.
Food
Bring the licensed caterer of your choice. Food can be served and kept warm here, but it cannot be prepared on site, because there is no commercial kitchen.
