What moves it

Three things set your number.

Guest count decides how much room and how many linens. Bar service is the biggest swing — a hosted full bar for 200 costs more than the room itself, while a cash bar costs you nothing. And the day of the week: a Saturday in June is our busiest slot, a Tuesday in February our quietest, and the price follows. Tell us those three and we’ll give you a real number, not a range.

The terms

Plainly, so nothing surprises you later.

Reservation fee
A non-refundable fee holds your date. It’s applied to your balance, not added on top — it’s money toward the event, not a deposit you lose.
Event insurance
A day-of event policy is required and purchased by you. It runs $100–$200 through most providers; we’ll point you to one.
Food
Bring in any licensed caterer. Food may be served and held here but not prepared on site — there’s no commercial kitchen.
Ready when you are

Tell us the date, the headcount, and the bar. We’ll send a real number.
