The Space

Brick, timber, and a whole lot of light

Arched windows run along two walls, original timber posts hold up the ceiling, and the hardwood floor glows when the afternoon sun comes in. It is a big, open room that still feels warm, whether you are seating forty or two hundred and fifty.

The details

Everything a planner asks first

The plain facts, before you walk the room. Anything we have not covered, just ask.

Ask a question
Size
About 4,500 square feet of open floor.
Capacity
Up to 250 guests.
Access
Second floor, with an elevator from the street entrance.
Included
Tables, chairs, linens, setup and teardown, an event attendant, and wifi.
In-house
A full bar, licensed and staffed by the venue. Plus a house sound system and projection.
Catering
Bring the licensed caterer of your choice. Food can be served and kept warm here, but there is no commercial kitchen for cooking on site.
Parking & load-in
A municipal lot behind the building and metered street parking on Washington Street. Rear load-in with elevator access for deliveries and rentals.
