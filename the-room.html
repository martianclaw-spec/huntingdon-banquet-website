Ask a question
The building
The second floor of the historic Miller Building, 708 Washington Street, in downtown Huntingdon.
Capacity
Up to 279 guests.
Size
About 4,500 square feet of open floor.
Access
Second floor, with an elevator from the street entrance.
Included
Tables, chairs, linens, setup and teardown, an event attendant, and wifi.
In-house
A full bar area, a convenient kitchenette, and a sound system. Exposed brick, arched windows, and the original hardwood floors come standard.
Catering
Bring the licensed caterer of your choice. The kitchenette gives them a place to stage, warm, and serve; there is no full commercial kitchen for cooking from scratch.
Parking & load-in
A municipal lot behind the building and metered street parking on Washington Street. Rear load-in with elevator access for deliveries and rentals.
